Our Story ☆

Founded in 2015, North Star Bakery was created to serve as a warm, welcoming star for our local neighborhood. We believe in slow baking, rich flavors, and bringing people together over delicious handmade food.

Our Sourcing Philosophy

We work directly with local flour mills and regional dairy farms. Every ingredient is selected with quality, sustainability, and community support in mind.

Meet Our Staff

Our small team of passionate bakers brings decades of combined experience in classic French and sourdough baking techniques.
